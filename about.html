About Benzeen_

Updated 17 October 2026

What it is

Benzeen_ is a free iPhone app for drivers in the UAE. It keeps petrol, Salik and Darb tolls, servicing, renewals and traffic fines in one place, works out what every kilometre really costs, and reminds you before a service, a renewal or a fine discount is due. It launched on the App Store in October 2026, and an Android version is on its way to Google Play.

Who makes it

Benzeen_ is built by an independent developer based in the UAE. It is not connected to any government body, toll operator or fuel retailer: not the RTA, Salik, ITC (Darb), ADNOC, ENOC or Emarat. Your data stays on your phone, and we have no servers that store it. The privacy policy has the details.

How the guides are written

The guides answer the questions UAE drivers search for most: this month's fuel prices, Salik and Darb rates, traffic fines and black points, Mulkiya renewal and service intervals. Every figure comes from an official source, linked at the end of each guide: the UAE Fuel Price Committee, Salik, ITC, the Ministry of Interior, the RTA and Dubai Police. Fuel prices change when the committee announces the next month's prices, and each guide shows the date its facts were last checked. Toll and fine figures are there to plan with; your Salik, Darb and police accounts are the official record.

How it's funded

The app is free and shows no ads. The guides are free to read and carry a small number of ads from Google, which pays for the time spent keeping them current. The ads never change what a guide says.

Found a mistake?

Prices and rules change. If a guide is out of date, email support@benzeen.app and we'll check it and fix it. For updates, follow @benzeen.app on Instagram.
