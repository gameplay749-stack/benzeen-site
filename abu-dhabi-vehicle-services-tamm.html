Abu Dhabi car services on TAMM

Updated 27 October 2026

In Abu Dhabi almost every car service is on TAMM, the government's one-stop platform: renewing the registration, transferring ownership, Darb tolls, residents' parking and paying fines. Here's what each costs, from TAMM's own service pages, and how to do it.

Abu Dhabi vehicle fees on TAMM

ServiceFee (AED)
Renew registration, private light vehicle350
Renew registration, private vehicle 3–12 tonnes400
Register a new private light vehicle400
Transfer ownership, private light vehicle350
Replace a lost or damaged registration card300
Register a vehicle with Darb100
Residents' parking permit (Mawaqif), first vehicle800
Residents' parking permit, second vehicle1,200

Renewal and transfer are the same price as in Dubai (AED 350 for a private car), but TAMM shows no separate AED 20 fee. A replacement registration card costs more: AED 300 against AED 70 in Dubai (see RTA fees).

How to renew your registration on TAMM

  1. Make sure the insurance is valid and, for an older car, that it has passed the vehicle test. TAMM's Vehicle Inspection Centre service lists the test centres.
  2. Clear any unpaid traffic fines. You can pay them on TAMM with no extra charge.
  3. Log in with UAE PASS, choose Renew Vehicle Registration and pay. You only need your Emirates ID.

The Mulkiya renewal guide compares the rules across emirates.

Darb tolls

Register your car with Darb on TAMM for AED 100, whether it's registered in Abu Dhabi or another emirate. Crossings cost AED 4 at peak times; see Salik and Darb rates for the hours and gates.

Mawaqif residents' parking

If you live in an area with paid Mawaqif parking, a residents' permit costs AED 800 for the first vehicle and AED 1,200 for a second. You need the tenancy contract, an electricity bill, the vehicle ownership and your Emirates ID, and you apply on TAMM with UAE PASS.

Traffic fines in Abu Dhabi

Pay Abu Dhabi Police fines on TAMM. Paying early earns a discount; the amounts and the discount rules are in our UAE traffic fines guide.

Questions

How much is car registration renewal in Abu Dhabi?

AED 350 for a private light vehicle on TAMM, and AED 400 for a private vehicle of 3 to 12 tonnes.

How much is ownership transfer in Abu Dhabi?

AED 350 for a private light vehicle.

How much does Darb registration cost?

AED 100 on TAMM, for cars registered in Abu Dhabi or any other emirate.

How much is a Mawaqif residents' parking permit?

AED 800 for the first vehicle and AED 1,200 for a second.

Can I pay Abu Dhabi traffic fines on TAMM?

Yes. Log in with UAE PASS; there's no extra charge for paying on TAMM.
