Car insurance renewal in the UAE

Updated 26 October 2026

Every car in the UAE needs valid insurance to be driven and to renew its Mulkiya. Prices aren't a free-for-all: the Central Bank sets a minimum and a maximum for every policy. Here's what renewal should cost, the discounts you're entitled to, and how to renew without a gap.

Comprehensive insurance price limits

For a comprehensive policy (your own car's damage plus third-party liability) the premium for 13 months must be:

  • Saloon: at least AED 1,300 and at most 5% of the car's value.
  • 4x4: at least AED 2,000 and at most 7% of the car's value.

A saloon worth AED 60,000 can't cost more than AED 3,000 to insure comprehensively. Insurers may not add any other charge unless the regulator allows it.

Discounts you can ask for at renewal

  • No-claims: 10% off the minimum premium after one claim-free year, 15% after two and 20% after three.
  • Loyalty: 10% off the minimum premium when you renew with the same insurer.
  • 50% off the minimum premium for medical workers, the armed forces and police, civil defence, People of Determination and drivers over 60.
  • Your current insurer must give you a free certificate of your claims history when you ask, so you can take your no-claims record to a new insurer.

Renewing without a gap

  1. Compare quotes a few weeks before the policy ends, with your claims-history certificate in hand.
  2. Check the cover: agency or garage repair, GCC or Oman cover, a hire car, roadside help. Each add-on costs extra.
  3. Renew before the old policy ends. Insurers send the policy to the traffic system electronically, so the car shows as insured straight away.
  4. Renew the Mulkiya next. In Dubai the insurance has to be valid for at least 6 months to renew the registration.

Driving without insurance is a fine of AED 500, 4 black points and 7 days' impoundment (see traffic fines).

Questions

What is the minimum price of car insurance in the UAE?

For a private saloon with 4 cylinders, third-party cover can't cost less than AED 750 for 13 months. Comprehensive cover starts at AED 1,300 for a saloon and AED 2,000 for a 4x4.

What is the most an insurer can charge for comprehensive cover?

5% of the car's value for a saloon and 7% for a 4x4, for a 13-month policy.

How much is the no-claims discount in the UAE?

10% of the minimum premium after one claim-free year, 15% after two and 20% after three. Renewing with the same insurer can add a 10% loyalty discount.

Can I renew my Mulkiya without insurance?

No. Valid insurance is required; in Dubai it must be valid for at least 6 months.

What is the fine for driving without insurance?

AED 500, 4 black points and 7 days' impoundment.
