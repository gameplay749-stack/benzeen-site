Dubai parking fines

Updated 25 October 2026

Dubai's public parking is run by Parkin. Forgetting to pay costs AED 150, more than a whole day of parking, and overstaying costs AED 100. Here are the fines, when parking is free, and how to pay or dispute a fine.

Dubai parking fines

Street and open-air parking, as listed in October 2026:

ViolationFine (AED)
Staying longer than the maximum time, or after your ticket runs out100
Parking without paying, or not showing the ticket where required150
Parking on the pavement or in a no-parking area200
Using a People of Determination space without a valid permit1,000
Parking in a space reserved for someone else1,000
Parking a car with no number plate1,000
Showing a car for sale or rent in public parking without a permit1,000

Multi-storey car parks have their own list: losing the ticket or card is AED 150, parking outside the bays or blocking an entrance is AED 200, and staying longer than allowed is AED 500.

When you pay for parking, and when it's free

  • Paid hours: 8 am to 10 pm, Monday to Saturday, in most zones. Some zones and multi-storey car parks charge 24 hours, as the sign shows.
  • Peak hours are 8–10 am and 4–8 pm. In premium areas, such as Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Deira and Jumeirah, peak parking costs AED 6 an hour before VAT (AED 6.30 with VAT since 1 June 2026). Off-peak prices didn't change.
  • Free: 10 pm to 8 am, and all day on Sundays and public holidays, outside the 24-hour zones.
  • Events: around big festivals, exhibitions and conferences, Parkin sets event rates in the surrounding zones, shown on signs.

How to pay a parking fine

On parkin.ae choose Pay Fines and search by plate (emirate, category, code and number), traffic file or fine number, or use the Parkin app. Parking fines also show with your other traffic fines, and unpaid ones must be cleared before you renew your Mulkiya.

How to dispute a parking fine

If you paid and still got a fine, the meter or app failed, or the sign was missing, raise a dispute in the Parkin app or on parkin.ae and attach your evidence: payment confirmation, dated photos of the bay and the sign. Parkin can refund a fine you already paid if the dispute succeeds. Do it soon after the fine is issued.

Sources: Parkin, variable parking tariff; Gulf News, Dubai parking fines list (15 October 2026); Gulf News on VAT from 1 June 2026. Parkin and the RTA are the official record; fine amounts can change.

Questions

What is the fine for not paying for parking in Dubai?

AED 150 for parking without paying, or without showing the ticket where one is required.

What is the fine for overstaying parking in Dubai?

AED 100 in street and open-air parking. In multi-storey car parks it's AED 500.

Is parking free in Dubai on Sundays?

Yes, in most zones parking is free all day on Sundays and public holidays, and every night from 10 pm to 8 am. Zones marked as 24-hour still charge.

What are Dubai parking peak hours?

8–10 am and 4–8 pm, Monday to Saturday. Premium areas charge AED 6 an hour at peak before VAT, AED 6.30 with VAT.

Can I dispute a Dubai parking fine?

Yes, through the Parkin app or parkin.ae, with evidence such as a payment confirmation or photos. A paid fine can be refunded if the dispute succeeds.
