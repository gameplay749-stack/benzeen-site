Fog and rain driving fines in the UAE

Updated 19 November 2026

Fog and rain bring their own fines in the UAE. Driving into a flowing valley costs AED 2,000, 23 black points and 60 days without your car, and just stopping to watch the water costs AED 1,000. Here is what each weather-related violation costs, how fog speed limits work, and what to do instead.

Fog and rain fines in the UAE

These are federal fines, so they apply in every emirate:

The valley and rescue-crew fines were added by the Ministry of Interior in May 2023. The others come from the federal fines schedule (Ministerial Resolution 178 of 2017). Police remind drivers of them every time a weather warning goes out.

Fog speed limits: 80 km/h when the signs say so

In fog, sandstorms or heavy rain, Abu Dhabi Police can drop the limit on main roads to 80 km/h. The lower limit shows on the electronic signs over the road and is announced on the police's social media, and while it shows it is the legal limit, whatever the fixed signs say. When visibility comes back, the normal limit returns. Speeding against the 80 km/h limit is fined like any other speeding:

Driving in fog

  • Low beams on, fog lights too if your car has them. High beams bounce off fog and make it worse.
  • No hazard lights while moving. They hide your indicators and confuse the drivers behind. Use them only once you've stopped safely off the road.
  • Leave a much bigger gap and don't overtake or change lanes unless you must.
  • If you can't see, leave the road at the next exit or a safe stopping place. Don't stop on the hard shoulder or in a lane.

Driving in heavy rain

  • Slow down early. The first rain lifts months of oil and dust off the road, and it gets slippery fast.
  • Stay out of flooded underpasses and wadis. You can't see how deep the water is or how fast it moves, and the fine applies whatever the level.
  • Don't stop to watch or film. Crowds near valleys and accidents block rescue crews, which is why both are fined.
  • Check wipers and tyres before the season. Driving on expired tyres is its own fine: AED 500, 4 black points and 7 days off the road.

Where the emirates differ

The amounts above are the same everywhere, but early-payment discounts and black-point rules depend on the emirate that issued the fine. Our UAE traffic fines guide explains the discounts in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah.

Questions

What is the fine for entering a valley during rain in the UAE?

AED 2,000, 23 black points and 60 days' impoundment of the car, whatever the water level.

Is there a fine for gathering near valleys when it rains?

Yes. Gathering near valleys, dams or flooded areas during rain costs AED 1,000 and 6 black points.

Can I use hazard lights while driving in fog?

No. Hazard lights while driving in fog or rain can be fined AED 500 and 4 black points. Use low beams, and hazard lights only once you've stopped safely.

What is the fine for driving in fog without lights?

AED 500 and 4 black points. The same fine applies for driving in fog against the authorities' instructions.

What is the speed limit in fog in Abu Dhabi?

When the electronic signs show it, 80 km/h on the affected roads. That limit replaces the fixed one until visibility returns, and speeding against it is fined the usual way.

Do these fines apply in Dubai and Sharjah too?

Yes. They're federal fines, the same in every emirate. Early-payment discounts depend on the emirate that issued the fine.
