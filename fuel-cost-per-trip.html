Use the grade your car needs

Most cars run on Special 95 or E-Plus 91; Super 98 costs more and only helps engines that ask for it. Check the fuel flap or your owner's manual. The fuel price guide has this month's prices and the history.

Questions

How do I calculate the fuel cost of a trip?

Divide the distance by your car's km/L to get the litres, then multiply by the price per litre. 140 km at 12 km/L is 11.7 litres.

How much is petrol per litre in the UAE in October 2026?

Super 98 AED 4.40, Special 95 AED 4.28, E-Plus 91 AED 4.21 and diesel AED 4.80.

What does 100 km of driving cost in fuel?

At 12 km/L with Special 95 it's about AED 35.67; at 8 km/L about AED 53.50.
