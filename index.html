How it works

  1. Add your car

    Scan the VIN on the windscreen or your Mulkiya. Make, model, engine and fuel grade fill in for you.

  2. Drive as usual

    Benzeen_ notices fuel stops and toll gates on its own, and asks one quick question at the pump.

  3. See every dirham

    Fuel efficiency in km/L, cost per km, spending by category, and a clear report every Monday.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and the app has no ads.

Is there an Android version?

It's coming soon to Google Play. Follow @benzeen.app on Instagram to hear when it's out.

Which emirates does it cover?

All seven, with every plate type. Salik and Darb tolls are counted automatically.

Is my data private?

It stays on your iPhone. The optional backup goes to your own iCloud.

Where can I get it?

On the App Store, free, for iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
