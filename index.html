Support

How does fuel-stop detection work?

With location set to Always, Benzeen notices when you stop at a fuel station for about 2.5 minutes and asks what you paid. Long-press the notification and reply “120” (dirhams) or “45 L full” — the official UAE price fills in the rest. Everything is processed on your iPhone.

How do service reminders work?

Benzeen uses your car maker's severe-conditions schedule (right for UAE heat and dust) and your real driving pace to predict each service, then reminds you a week before, on the day, and follows up if it hasn't been logged. You can change any interval under Garage › your car › Edit.

Moving to a new iPhone

Sign in with Apple in Settings › Account. Your data is backed up to your private iCloud and comes back when you log in on the new iPhone. Without an account, use Settings › Your data › Export backup.

Deleting your data

Settings › Account › Delete account removes your iCloud backup and everything on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
