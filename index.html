How it works

  1. Add your car

    Scan the VIN on the windscreen or your Mulkiya. Make, model, engine and fuel grade fill in for you.

  2. Drive as usual

    Benzeen_ notices fuel stops and toll gates on its own, and asks one quick question at the pump.

  3. See every dirham

    Fuel efficiency in km/L, cost per km, spending by category, and a clear report every Monday.
