Petrol vs diesel vs hybrid: running costs in the UAE

Updated 7 November 2026

Diesel costs more than petrol per litre in the UAE: in November 2026 it's AED 4.75 against AED 4.04 for Special 95. So a diesel car only saves money if it goes at least 18% further on a litre. When does a diesel car pay off?

Divide the diesel price by the petrol price. In November 2026 that's 4.75 ÷ 4.04, so a diesel car needs about 18% more km/L than a Special 95 car just to break even, and about 20% more than an E-Plus 91 car. A petrol car doing 10 km/L is matched by a diesel doing 11.8 km/L; anything better than that saves money, anything worse costs more.

Few private cars on sale in the UAE run on diesel; most are pickups and large SUVs. Compare the same kind of car, not a diesel pickup with a petrol sedan.

Hybrids: same petrol, fewer litres

A regular hybrid fills up with the same petrol at the same price, so it saves exactly in proportion to how much further it goes on a litre. With the example figures above, 2,000 km a month on Special 95 costs AED 808 at 10 km/L and AED 505 at 16 km/L. Hybrids save most in stop-start city traffic, where the electric motor does more of the work, and least on long, fast highway runs.

What the calculator leaves out

Fuel is only part of the cost. The price of the car, insurance, servicing and resale value can matter as much. Salik and Darb, parking and registration fees don't depend on the fuel your car uses.

Questions

Is diesel cheaper than petrol in the UAE?

No. In November 2026 diesel is AED 4.75 a litre, against AED 4.04 for Special 95 and AED 3.97 for E-Plus 91. Diesel has cost more than Special 95 in each of the last 8 months.

When is a diesel car cheaper to run?

Only when it goes about 18% further on a litre than the petrol car you'd buy instead, at November 2026 prices. A petrol car at 10 km/L is matched by a diesel at 11.8 km/L.

How much does a hybrid save on fuel?

It uses the same petrol, so the saving matches its km/L gain: a car going 60% further on a litre spends 37.5% less on fuel. Hybrids gain most in city traffic.

How do I find my car's km/L?

Fill the tank, reset the trip meter, and at the next full fill divide the kilometres driven by the litres you put in. Or log fill-ups in Benzeen_ and it does this for you.

Do diesel or hybrid cars pay less Salik or registration?

No. Salik and Darb charge the same per crossing whatever the fuel, and registration fees depend on the type of vehicle, not its fuel.
