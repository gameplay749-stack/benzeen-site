Questions

When are UAE fuel prices announced?

The UAE Fuel Price Committee announces the next month's prices on the last day of each month, and they take effect on the 1st. November 2026 prices are due around the end of October; this page is updated when they're announced.

Are petrol prices the same at every station?

Yes. ADNOC, ENOC and Emarat all charge the same official price per litre, in every emirate.

How are UAE fuel prices set?

Since August 2015, retail fuel prices in the UAE follow global market prices. The committee reviews them every month, so they can go up or down.

Which petrol grade does my car need?

Use the grade your car maker recommends, shown inside the fuel flap or in the owner's manual. A higher grade costs more without helping an engine that doesn't need it.

How much is a full tank in October 2026?

For a 60-litre sedan tank, about AED 257 with Special 95, AED 253 with E-Plus 91 and AED 264 with Super 98.
